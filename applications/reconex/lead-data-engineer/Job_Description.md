# Reconex — Lead Data Engineer

**Location:** Hybrid · Solon, OH  
**Type:** Full-Time  

## Overview
We’re looking for a hands-on Lead Data Engineer to own the data platform behind Reconex’s freight intelligence products. Everything we build for our customers runs on data, and you’ll be the person who makes that data fast, reliable, and trustworthy.

## About the Role
You’ll own our Databricks lakehouse end to end: ingestion, transformation, orchestration, performance, and cost. You’ll also wear the DBA hat for our operational databases. This is a lead role on a lean team, so you’ll set the standards, make the architecture calls, and stay hands-on in the code. We’re an AI-forward engineering team: tools like Claude Code are part of how we work, and we expect you to use them to multiply your output.

## What You’ll Do
- Design, build, and operate reliable ETL/ELT pipelines in Databricks (Spark, Delta Lake, Workflows) ingesting shipment, carrier, and invoice data.
- Own database administration for our operational systems: schema design, index and query tuning, capacity planning, backup/recovery, and access control.
- Plan and execute modernization strategy for our transactional databases.
- Establish data quality checks, observability, and alerting so bad data is caught before customers ever see it.
- Model data for reporting and partner with product and engineering on customer-facing analytics and dashboards.
- Manage platform performance and cost, including cluster sizing, job optimization, and storage layout.
- Use AI development tools (Claude Code or similar) daily for building, refactoring, testing, and documenting data systems.
- Set engineering standards for the data domain: version control, CI/CD for data code, testing, and documentation.
- Over time, lead analytics initiatives and lay the groundwork for data science, including feature pipelines and experimentation support.

## What We’re Looking For
- 5+ years in data engineering (or adjacent roles) with production ownership of data pipelines.
- Deep Databricks and Spark experience, including Delta Lake, PySpark, Spark SQL, and job orchestration.
- Expert SQL and solid DBA fundamentals on relational databases (SQL Server, PostgreSQL, or similar).
- Strong Python skills.
- Experience running data workloads in the cloud (Azure preferred).
- Fluency with AI-assisted development: you’re comfortable making agentic tools like Claude Code part of your daily workflow, and you’re curious about where they can take you next.
- Self-directed and accountable, comfortable owning the data domain and communicating clearly with non-technical stakeholders.

## Nice to Have
- Logistics, supply chain, or freight data experience (TMS platforms, carrier invoicing).
- BI tooling such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Exposure to statistics, ML, or data science workflows (MLflow, feature engineering).
- Streaming experience (Structured Streaming, Kafka, or Event Hubs).
- Infrastructure-as-code and data governance (Unity Catalog).

## Why Reconex
- Direct impact: your pipelines power the product our customers use every day.
- Modern stack and an in-house development culture that ships.
- An AI-forward team that invests in tools like Claude Code.
- Hybrid flexibility from our Solon, OH office.
